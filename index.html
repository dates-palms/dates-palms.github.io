🏠 Welcome to the Decision Support System

An advanced AI tool for informed decision-making in date palm pruning and thinning.

How to use the system?

1

Step 1: Data Entry

Go to the Data Entry page. Fill in the tree age, thinning protocol (general or by generation), and the climate data for each physiological period.

2

Step 2: Smart Analysis

After filling all fields, click the "Analyze & Generate Prediction" button. The system will process your data using an XGBoost model with 2000 decision trees.

3

Step 3: Get Results

The system will display a yield prediction distribution chart with confidence intervals. Use this to guide your thinning decisions at the April–May decision node.
